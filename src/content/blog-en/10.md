**Shockwave therapy** employs specialized equipment to relieve muscle tension caused by repetitive strain. The device transmits shock waves into painful areas to stimulate new micro-injuries in affected muscles and tendons, triggering the body's natural tissue repair process. It also reduces pain-signaling neurotransmitters while promoting pain-relieving substance release.

## Suitable Candidates

1. Patients with chronic joint pain (jumper's knee, painful shoulder, tennis elbow, heel spur, shin splints, hip pain)
2. Office workers with repetitive strain injuries causing myofascial pain syndrome

## Treatment Frequency

Approximately **once weekly**, with a minimum of 3–4 sessions depending on pain severity — allowing the body recovery time between sessions.

## Post-Treatment Care

- Apply cold compresses or ice massage to manage soreness
- Perform gentle stretching to reduce muscle inflammation
- Avoid massage immediately after treatment — shockwave intentionally triggers inflammation to promote healing

## Contraindications

Not suitable for: individuals under 18, pregnant women, those with blood clotting disorders, patients on anticoagulants, pacemaker recipients, active cancer patients, and those with skin infections or open wounds.
