**Dementia** is a group of symptoms from various brain abnormalities that impair cognitive abilities including memory, language skills, comprehension, decision-making, and social interaction.

## Brain Regions and Their Symptoms

| Region | Effect When Damaged |
|---|---|
| Front Brain | Memory and behavioral control |
| Temporal Lobe | Emotions, behavior, language and communication |
| Central Brain | Intelligence, reading, reasoning, orientation |
| Rear Brain | Vision |
| Cerebellum | Coordination and muscle function |
| Brain Stem | Breathing, heart rate, swallowing, blood pressure |

## Types of Dementia

**Treatable forms** stem from physical conditions like cerebrovascular disease, tumors, or vitamin B12 deficiency.

**Irreversible forms** (80% in elderly patients) include **Alzheimer's disease** (approximately 50% of dementia patients), Parkinson's disease, and certain infections.

## Prevention Strategies

Adequate sleep, balanced nutrition, controlling blood pressure and cholesterol, avoiding alcohol and tobacco, and regular cognitive stimulation can help slow cognitive decline progression.
