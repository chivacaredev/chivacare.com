## Massage

Uses techniques of squeezing, pressing, applying pressure to acupuncture points, and applying heat through herbal compresses or natural stones.

**Benefits:** Stimulates blood circulation, reduces muscle tension, alleviates fatigue, promotes relaxation and emotional calmness, and improves sleep quality.

## Physical Therapy

Begins with a preliminary consultation, physical examination, diagnosis, and treatment plan development — using physical therapy equipment combined with muscle stretching and targeted exercise.

**Benefits:** Treats body pain appropriately for acute and chronic phases, restores normal body function, strengthens muscles and movement capacity, and teaches injury prevention.

## Which Is Right for You?

- **Massage** suits those seeking muscle relaxation and relief from initial muscle tension and soreness
- **Physical therapy** benefits those with chronic pain, needing muscle stretching, or recovering from sports injuries
