Muscle weakness — ranging from mild to severe — can affect muscles throughout the body across all genders and ages. Delaying treatment or neglecting physical therapy significantly increases the risk of **muscle atrophy**.

## Causes

The deterioration of motor nerve cells causes muscles to gradually shrink, affecting the hands, arms, and legs. Initially symptoms appear on one side — difficulty gripping objects, frequent stumbling, struggling to stand — before progressively affecting both sides.

In severe cases: leg and hand paralysis, slurred speech, choking while eating, and respiratory muscle weakness.

## How to Recognize Symptoms

- Arm and leg muscle weakness
- Difficulty swallowing
- Voice changes
- Progressive muscle atrophy and fasciculations

## Treatment Recommendations

Patients experiencing these symptoms should promptly consult a physician for accurate diagnosis. Treatment options include medication, exercise, and physical therapy. Working with physical therapists helps prevent atrophy from prolonged muscle disuse.
