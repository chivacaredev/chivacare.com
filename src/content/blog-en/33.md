Knee osteoarthritis is a common problem, particularly among elderly populations, significantly impacting daily functioning. The condition makes standing, walking, and climbing stairs difficult. However, proper self-care can help prevent premature onset.

## Prevention Methods

- **Weight management:** Knees bear the body's primary weight load — maintaining a healthy weight reduces joint stress significantly
- **Posture adjustments:** Avoid prolonged sitting in one position
- **Supplements:** Glucosamine and chondroitin support cartilage health in early stages
- **Avoid frequent steroid use:** Steroids can weaken bone and accelerate degeneration

## Exercise Regimen for Knee Protection

1. **Position 1:** Lie supine with pillow under knee, straighten leg while tensing quadriceps, hold counting 1–10, repeat 10 times
2. **Position 2:** Sit upright, extend leg forward, hold counting 1–5, repeat 10 times
3. **Position 3:** Stand holding chair back, kick leg backward, repeat 10 times

Begin these preventive measures today — the earlier you start, the better protected your knees will be as you age.
