Chivacare introduces five free smartphone applications designed to enhance brain function and help prevent Alzheimer's disease. These games combine entertainment with cognitive development.

## 1. Ball Sort Puzzle
Players move colored balls within glass bottles so each bottle contains only one color. Strengthens planning abilities and observation skills.

## 2. 2048
Combine numbered tiles with matching values to reach 2048. Develops strategic thinking and working memory.

## 3. Brick Ball Crusher
Shoot balls to destroy brick formations within limited shots, requiring tactical planning and spatial awareness.

## 4. Unblock Me
Slide wooden blocks to free a red block from confinement. Develops problem-solving and planning skills.

## 5. Sudoku
A classic brain development game suitable for all ages. Regular Sudoku practice improves logical reasoning and short-term memory.

## Why Brain Training Matters

Regular cognitive stimulation through puzzles and games can help maintain neural connections and slow cognitive decline. Combined with physical exercise, proper nutrition, quality sleep, and regular medical check-ups, these apps form one part of a comprehensive brain health strategy.
