**Peripheral Magnetic Stimulation (PMS)** represents innovative technology employing electromagnetic waves to stimulate various body areas for treating pain and numbness while effectively promoting tissue and nerve repair.

## Benefits of PMS Treatment

- Treats both symptoms and causes of acute, subacute, and chronic pain
- Addresses nerve-related and non-nerve pain including muscle, tendon, and bone discomfort
- Stimulates tissue repair processes
- Promotes healing of damaged nerves, including nerve compression from spinal conditions
- Encourages neuroplasticity to normalize abnormal nerve behavior
- Reduces or eliminates numbness
- Increases muscle strength, even in paralyzed or weakened muscles

## Who Benefits from PMS Treatment?

- Office syndrome and various pain conditions
- Numbness from nerve compression or diabetic complications
- Sports or accident injuries affecting muscles, tendons, and bones
- Spinal conditions and herniated discs
- Paralysis or stroke patients (reduces spasticity)
