**Text Neck Syndrome** refers to a group of pain symptoms in the neck, shoulder, and back muscles that may include vertebral degeneration and cervical disc issues — stemming from prolonged forward-flexed posture caused by smartphone, tablet, or wireless device usage.

## Common Symptoms

- Chronic pain in the head, neck, shoulders, or upper back
- In severe cases: numbness, radiating pain down the arms or lower back
- Arm and hand weakness or postural changes

## The Physics of Forward Head Posture

Every **15-degree forward head tilt** increases cervical spine pressure by approximately **27 kilograms**. A typical smartphone user holds their head at 45–60 degrees for hours daily.

## Prevention Methods

1. **Posture:** Maintain neutral neck alignment. Raise arms instead of bending the neck when using devices
2. **Usage Duration:** Take breaks every 10–15 minutes during prolonged use
3. **Strengthening Exercises:** Chin tucks, backward head pressing, and elbow retraction — hold 7 seconds, 5 reps, 3 sets
