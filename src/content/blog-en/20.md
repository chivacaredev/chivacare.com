Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis) — if neglected — requires increasingly invasive intervention. Research indicates that patients who receive physical therapy training for **3–6 months** can recover without surgery.

## The Four Stages

1. **Painful Stage (0–3 months):** Primarily shoulder pain, with slight stiffness
2. **Freezing Stage (3–9 months):** Increased stiffness with persistent pain when moving
3. **Frozen Stage (9–15 months):** Stiffness pronounced; pain diminishes but movement is severely restricted
4. **Thawing Stage (15–24 months):** Movement gradually improves; most patients fully recover with proper therapy
