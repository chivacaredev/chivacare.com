Despite comprehensive modern medical treatment, some stroke patients experience persistent muscle weakness and spasticity. Research shows approximately **30% of patients** still cannot walk independently even after six months of rehabilitation therapy.

## How TMS Helps

TMS operates by delivering electromagnetic waves to stimulate the brain externally. It can stimulate the affected brain hemisphere or inhibit the opposite side to restore neural balance — accelerating neural reconnection, promoting better movement recovery, and reducing muscle spasticity.

> "TMS treatment requires continuous sessions of at least twice weekly — approximately 5–10 sessions or more before results become apparent."

## Safety and Contraindications

TMS demonstrates high safety with minimal side effects. However, the procedure **cannot** be performed on patients with:

- Frequent seizures
- Metal implants in the brain (such as aneurysm clips)
- Electronic implants (such as pacemakers)
