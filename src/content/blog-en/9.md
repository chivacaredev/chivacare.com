**Piriformis syndrome** occurs when the piriformis muscle, located deep in the buttock, compresses or irritates the sciatic nerve. Individuals experiencing pain when applying pressure to the buttocks region and lower back discomfort may have this condition.

## Who Is at Risk?

This syndrome is particularly common among those who spend extended periods sitting in office environments. Prolonged hip flexion from sedentary work positions creates vulnerability to piriformis tightening and sciatic nerve irritation.

## Symptoms

- Deep buttock pain, especially when sitting for extended periods
- Pain that radiates down the back of the thigh and leg
- Discomfort when climbing stairs or walking uphill
- Tingling or numbness in the buttock and leg

## Treatment Approach

Treatment combines physical therapy techniques including manual therapy, targeted stretching of the piriformis and hip rotators, strengthening exercises for the gluteal muscles, and postural correction. For persistent cases, shockwave therapy or dry needling may be incorporated.
