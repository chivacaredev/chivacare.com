**Dry Needling** is a western approach where practitioners insert fine needles into painful areas to enhance blood circulation. This technique targets trigger points — using the needle tip to stimulate muscle knots and encourage relaxation.

As muscles release tension, knots diminish in size, circulation improves, and pain gradually subsides.

## How It Differs from Traditional Acupuncture

Traditional Chinese acupuncture follows meridian theory and stimulates points that may be distant from the pain site.

Dry Needling is based on **Western anatomy and neuroscience**, targeting specific muscular trigger points identified through physical examination — particularly effective for **myofascial pain syndrome**.

## Who Benefits?

- Patients with myofascial pain syndrome
- Chronic neck, shoulder, and back pain sufferers
- Athletes with repetitive strain injuries
- Office workers with persistent tension from prolonged sitting
- Patients seeking drug-free pain management alternatives
