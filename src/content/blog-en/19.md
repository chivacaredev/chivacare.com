Back pain from prolonged standing involves sustained contraction of lower back muscles, potentially causing sharp pain that may radiate down to the legs. Without proper treatment, this can progress to chronic low back pain, plantar fasciitis, and joint stiffness in the hips and knees.

## Shockwave Therapy Treatment

Shockwave therapy involves directing shock waves to painful areas. ChivaCare combines expertise from rehabilitation medicine specialists and neurologists to design personalized treatment plans using high-performance equipment.

## Conditions Treated

- Chronic pain conditions
- Plantar fasciitis and heel pain
- Office syndrome, frozen shoulder, back and shoulder pain
- Tendinopathy and muscle trigger points
- Hip pain
