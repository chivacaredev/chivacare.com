**TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation)** involves directing magnetic waves through the skull to stimulate the brain's cortex directly — specifically the left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC). This triggers neurotransmitter changes through neuroplasticity, activating underactive brain regions and potentially increasing serotonin levels.

## FDA Approval and Effectiveness

The U.S. FDA approved TMS for treating treatment-resistant depression in **2008**. Research demonstrates clear therapeutic results in **40–50% of cases**, reducing long-term medication side effects and acute suicide rates.

## TMS Versus Other Treatments

TMS offers significant advantages over ECT (Electroconvulsive Therapy):

- No surgery required
- Fewer side effects
- Superior efficacy in treatment-resistant cases
- Lower cost

## Indications for TMS

1. Treatment-resistant depression unresponsive to antidepressants
2. Severe depression with high suicidal risk
3. Inability to tolerate antidepressant medication
4. Candidates for ECT who cannot accept its adverse effects
