**TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation)** transmits magnetic waves through the skull to the brain region causing disease. These electromagnetic energies stimulate brain cells by activating brain function, triggering neurotransmitter release, suppressing symptoms to promote healing, and enhancing blood circulation.

## Conditions Treatable with TMS

- Recovery from paralysis and stroke caused by cerebrovascular disease
- Restoration of diminished memory function
- Depression treatment and rehabilitation
- Parkinson's disease management
- Migraine headache relief
- Chronic pain management
- Neurological, brain, and muscle disorders

TMS is also an alternative for patients unresponsive to pharmaceutical interventions.

## Is TMS Safe?

TMS received **USFDA approval**, confirming high safety standards with no adverse effects on surrounding organs. Side effects remain rare: mild auditory disturbance during treatment, slight post-treatment dizziness, and facial muscle twitching.

No medications or surgery are required; patients can return home immediately after treatment.

## Treatment Duration

Each session lasts **30–60 minutes**, with frequency of 1–3 times per week depending on individual conditions.
