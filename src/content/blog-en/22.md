**TMS Therapy (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation)** is a non-invasive treatment using electromagnetic waves comparable to MRI technology. The procedure transmits energy through the skull to the brain, inducing electrical currents within brain cells and altering the electrical potential of nerve cells — modifying their function through stimulation or inhibition.

## How TMS Treats Depression

TMS specifically targets the **left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC)** — the region most associated with mood regulation and depressive disorders. When this region is underactive (as in depression), TMS stimulation restores normal activity levels, promoting the natural production of serotonin and dopamine.

## Advantages Over Traditional Treatment

- Non-invasive — no surgery, anesthesia, or hospitalization required
- No systemic side effects like those associated with antidepressant medications
- Outpatient procedure — patients can return to normal activities immediately
- **FDA-approved** for treatment-resistant depression since 2008
