**Shin splints (medial tibial stress syndrome)** are characterized by pain along the inner side and ridge of the shin bone, commonly observed in athletes, particularly runners.

## Causes

The most frequent cause is tight and weak calf muscles. When calf muscles become less effective at absorbing impact forces, those forces transfer through the shin bone, triggering inflammation and pain.

Additional contributing factors:
- Sudden increase in training volume or intensity
- Running on hard surfaces
- Worn or inappropriate footwear
- Overpronation (flat feet)
- Muscle imbalances in the lower leg

## Simple Calf Muscle Exercises

1. **Heel raises:** 10 reps × 4 sets — strengthens calf muscles and improves shock absorption
2. **Split squats with heel raises:** 10 reps × 4 sets — develops functional lower leg strength
3. **Calf muscle stretching:** Hold 15 seconds × 10 reps — maintains flexibility and prevents recurrence
