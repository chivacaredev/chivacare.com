**TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation)** uses electromagnetic waves to stimulate the brain and balance brain function. When experiencing migraine pain, the brain generates cortical spreading depression (CSD) waves that cause aura symptoms and pain. TMS releases electromagnetic waves to reduce these migraine waves and decrease pain sensations.

## FDA Approval for Migraine Treatment

TMS is the **first device FDA-approved** for migraine treatment. Research shows it effectively:

- Reduces headache pain intensity
- Decreases pain frequency
- Prevents migraine recurrence

**During acute pain episodes:** Stimulation reduces cortical spreading depression, rapidly decreasing pain.  
**For prevention:** Stimulation addresses dishabituation in migraine, reducing recurrence rates.

## Advantages of TMS for Migraine

- High safety with no pain generation and low side effects
- Treats headaches, migraines, tension headaches, and chronic headaches
- Reduces medication consumption and medication-related side effects
- Continuous treatment improves headache severity and prevents recurrence
- Treatment administered by brain and nervous system specialists

## Other Conditions Treatable with TMS

Stroke rehabilitation, cerebrovascular disease, Parkinson's disease, depression, and Alzheimer's disease.
