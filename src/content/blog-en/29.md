Carpal tunnel syndrome occurs frequently among computer workers. Continuous computer use and extended mouse handling keeps the wrist in an extended position for prolonged periods, leading to nerve compression. It also appears in those who repeatedly flex and extend the wrist, or perform work involving prolonged hand and arm vibration.

## Symptoms

Pain and numbness in the palm and fingers, typically affecting one or both hands — especially the **thumb, index, middle, and half of the ring finger**. Weakness in hand grip strength causes dropped objects.

Without treatment, muscle atrophy becomes visible. Symptoms may intensify at night, though shaking the wrist may provide temporary relief.

## Treatment Options

1. Modifying wrist use patterns and work ergonomics
2. Wearing a wrist brace, especially at night
3. Physical therapy targeting inflammation and nerve decompression
4. Surgical intervention may be necessary if nerve compression persists

Early treatment prevents permanent nerve damage and preserves hand function.
