Are you experiencing stress, depression, loss of appetite, insomnia, and anxiety, requiring medication just to sleep? **TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation)** offers an alternative solution for those who prefer not to rely on medication.

## How Are Depression and Insomnia Related?

- Depression causes insomnia, incomplete sleep, and waking early without feeling refreshed
- Insomnia deteriorates quality of life, potentially triggering depression
- Sleep deprivation affects brain chemical levels associated with mood regulation
- Nighttime insomnia increases negative thinking and triggers depressive episodes

People with insomnia face **ten times greater** depression risk than sound sleepers.

## How Does TMS Treat Depression and Insomnia?

The FDA approved TMS for treatment-resistant major depressive disorder. Research indicates **40–50% of patients** show improvement.

TMS sends magnetic waves through the skull, directly stimulating the left prefrontal cortex (DLPFC), inducing electrical currents in emotion-regulating brain cells. This triggers neurotransmitter changes and neuroplasticity modifications.

## Research Evidence

A 2019 systematic review found TMS more effective and cost-efficient than medications and ECT. Optimal results come from high-frequency stimulation — 3,000 waves per session, five sessions weekly for 4–6 weeks.
