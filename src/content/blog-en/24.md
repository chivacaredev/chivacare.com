The knee joint bears the body's weight during nearly all daily activities. Without prompt treatment, knee pain can progress to osteoarthritis.

## Knee Pain Has Multiple Causes

**Ligament Issues:** Repetitive stress from athletics can inflame or tear ligaments. Severe trauma may rupture the anterior cruciate ligament, causing swelling and instability.

**Cartilage Damage:** Poor hip and thigh muscle strength while climbing stairs or from prolonged sitting contributes to cartilage wear.

**Meniscus Problems:** Meniscus deterioration from age-related wear leads to progressive cartilage erosion. Younger individuals may also experience meniscal tears from trauma.

**Osteoarthritis:** Often resulting from untreated injuries to ligaments or meniscus — pain typically begins inside the knee and spreads as severity increases.

## Symptoms Requiring Medical Attention

- Abnormal knee pain with restricted movement
- Swelling, heat, and pain indicating inflammation
- Persistent pain unresponsive to home remedies

## Prevention Strategies

Strengthen thigh and hip muscles through straight leg raises, squats, and lunges. Maintain healthy weight and avoid risky positions like prolonged kneeling or deep knee bends.
