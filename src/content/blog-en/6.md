Many people dismiss early symptoms as normal tiredness — but recognizing warning signs early can prevent serious joint damage.

## 7 Dangerous Warning Signs

1. Pain in the joints
2. Swollen joints
3. Feeling stiffness in the joints (especially in the morning)
4. Tenderness upon pressure
5. Difficulty moving arms and legs — some activities become impossible to perform
6. Joint sounds or noises (clicking, popping, or grinding)
7. These symptoms persist for **three or more days**

## When to Seek Professional Help

If you have **three or more** of the above symptoms, consult a physician or physical therapist for proper evaluation and treatment.
