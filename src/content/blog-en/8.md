Surgery comprises only one aspect of medical treatment — patients must begin post-operative rehabilitation to ensure proper recovery and return to their previous lifestyle.

## Why Rehabilitation Must Start Immediately

During recovery, the body repairs injured tissues through tissue formation at the surgical site, which can develop into **scar tissue** causing joint stiffness and muscle adhesion. Delayed rehabilitation risks:

- Joint contracture and prolonged recovery
- Muscle weakness from disuse atrophy
- Scar tissue adhesions limiting movement

## The Primary Role of Physical Therapists

Following surgery, physical therapists design customized exercise programs addressing strength and mobility. For patients with spinal cord or brain involvement, **occupational therapists** provide neurological stimulation therapy in addition to physical rehabilitation.

## Recovery Duration

Patients generally experience noticeable improvement within **4–6 weeks**, though comprehensive post-operative rehabilitation remains essential for full recovery. Duration depends on disease specifics, rehabilitation plans, and individual healing rates.
