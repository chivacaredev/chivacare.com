Cerebrovascular disease is a leading cause of hemiplegia and hemiparesis, preventing patients from performing daily activities. Delayed treatment risks permanent disability.

## Common Symptoms After Stroke

- Mild to severe paralysis with limited or no limb movement
- Facial drooping, slurred speech, and difficulty swallowing
- Numbness in affected limbs
- Muscle stiffness and spasticity
- Swallowing difficulties increasing aspiration risk

## What is TMS?

**TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation)** uses electromagnetic wave pulses to induce changes in brain, nervous system, and muscle function — without injury or radiation — helping restore nervous system and muscle deficiencies damaged by stroke.

## How TMS Treats Stroke

The device stimulates brain tissue 1–3 cm deep, inducing nervous system and muscle changes. It can stimulate the affected hemisphere or inhibit the opposite side for balance — stimulating neural reconnection for improved movement and recovery.

> Combined with physical therapy, TMS-assisted stroke rehabilitation has demonstrated improvements of **50–60%** in patient functional outcomes.
